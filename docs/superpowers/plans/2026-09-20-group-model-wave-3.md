# Group model — Wave 3 (Groups become the model) + issue graph (2026-09-20, revised 2026-09-21)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development. On approval this file is copied to `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-20-group-model-wave-3.md` and executed task-by-task (one implementer per task, task review after each, one whole-wave review at the end). Acceptance is by mutation (revert one function or rule → a named assertion must fail), not by a green suite. Every dispatch carries the bounded-command rule (no single command over ~8 minutes) and the stack-don't-review rule for dobrerares' PRs. Merge-as-you-go for PRs against `main`.

**Goal:** Make Groups the real model of OSUBB — the frontend reads Groups, `groups`/`group_members` become the write master through atomic Group commands, the Administrare area exists for BC and for Group Managers, and the six legacy structure tables, the origin bridge, the `event_scope` enum, `is_interne`, `departments.kind` and level 4 are gone — while the promotion machinery waits for Wave 4.

**Architecture — five lanes, reads first, then writers, then drop (decision 4):**

### Rulings added by the second grilling (2026-09-20 evening; recorded 2026-09-21)

- **R20 No `move_group` — a Group's parent is fixed at creation.** "We will never move a Group." A wrongly placed Group is archived and created again. This keeps Department Cup attribution simple: standings walk each Task's live `path` and no path ever changes. Consequences: T9's `move_group` row is struck; Decision 5's re-parenting clause is struck; the cross-cutting "one re-parent per statement" rule and `group_structure_races.test.sql`'s re-parent race are struck; `validate_group_path`'s UPDATE arm and `cascade_group_path` stay as tripwires rather than as machinery; `docs/backend/conventions.md` §10 states the immutability and T23 asserts it. (ADR-0009 amended 2026-09-20; `CONTEXT.md` **Child Group**.)
- **R21 Archiving refuses on open work.** `archive_group` raises `PT409 group_has_open_work` while the Group or any descendant holds a Task that is not `completed`, `unfulfilled` or `cancelled`, or a pending Completed-work Request. Archiving never cancels a Task — the Manager finishes or cancels it first, with the reasons the Tracker already requires. It does settle what has no executor, in the same transaction: pending Applications on the subtree are declined (actor = the archiver, note "Grup arhivat", applicants notified), and future Events of the subtree are cancelled with the same reason (ADR-0008 cancellation semantics; past Events stay as history) — **ruled 2026-09-21:** through `private.cancel_event_impl`, not the public wrapper, whose own gate would refuse a Group Manager who is not an Organization Event's creator; the archiver is already authorized for the whole subtree, and no new error code is minted; then the status cascades to every descendant in one statement (R19). Replaces the first draft, which would have stranded an Executor's `in_review` Task behind `can_manage_group_work`.
- **R22 Membership Status never edits rosters.** Deactivating a Member leaves every `group_members` row and Group Role in place — authority is already live-filtered by the Wave 2 helpers, so an inactive Manager cannot act, and a reactivated Member resumes exactly the positions they held. `set_member_status` therefore touches no roster row in either direction, and says so in a `comment on function`. Rosters change by hand only through T10's three commands, which refuse an **inactive** target with `group_member_not_eligible`. Administrare's Roster tab shows each Member's Membership Status beside their Group Role so a Manager removes or keeps them deliberately.
- **R23 Falling below a Group's Minimum Level ends membership of that Group.** The gate binds joining, seeing, and staying. Two directions, one invariant:
  - **Demotion (automatic).** When `set_member_role` (T7/#580) puts the target below the Minimum Level of a Group where they hold any `group_members` row, the command deletes those rows — ordinary membership and Group Roles on that Group alike — in the same transaction, rows locked `for update`, and withdraws their pending Applications on such Groups. A Group Role held on an **ancestor** with a lower Minimum Level is untouched and still flows down. The Notification to the target names the Groups left.
  - **A raised Minimum Level (confirmed).** Both update commands (T9/#582) refuse with `PT409 group_has_members_below_level` (the message carries the count) unless `p_confirm_removals` is true; then they delete exactly those rows, withdraw those Applications, notify each removed Member and send one summary to the Group's Managers. Descendants are never touched — `group_min_level_above_children` already refuses a level above a child's. The interface (T15/#588) lists who will leave before it sets the flag, so a stale form can never remove anyone by accident.
  - Together these make T13's `23514 group_member_below_min_level` invariant **total**: after `db reset` and after the seed, a roster row below its Group's Minimum Level cannot exist, and the suite asserts the count is 0.
- **R24 The Moderator seat is transferable.** Moderator is a Role held by the IT Coordinator on their own address, not a fixed account: the sitting Moderator grants `moderator` to the successor, who then removes it from the predecessor. Nobody may change their own Role or Status, and **only a Moderator may change the Role or Status of a Member who currently holds `bc` or `moderator`** (so BC cannot deactivate a Moderator), which is what makes the seat impossible to empty by its holder or by BC. The first accounts on a fresh production database are created once by a service-role bootstrap script (#112); every later account comes through invite, CSV import, or Administrare. (ADR-0003 and ADR-0004, amended 2026-09-20.)
- **R25 Commands notify the target, never the actor.** Every roster and Role command writes one direct `system` Notification to the **target** through `private.notify`, in the same transaction: added to a Group, removed from a Group, appointed under a position's display name or the position removed (T10/#583, `p_link = '/grupuri/<group_id>'`); the new Role, naming the Adunarea Generală seat when it is Drept de Vot and listing any Groups left (T7/#580). `set_member_status` writes **none** — a deactivated Member cannot read it and reactivation is silent. The link target `/grupuri/:id` is a member-facing page built in T16 (#589).
- **R26 Group Audience is one server helper.** `private.group_audience(p_group_id)` (T24/#601) is the single implementation of `CONTEXT.md`'s **Group Audience**: every active Member with a roster row on any Group whose `path` contains the argument, plus, for every such Group with Automatic Membership, every active Member at or above that Group's Minimum Level. Automatic Membership is never materialized, so nothing that fans out may join `group_members` directly. `private.event_notification_recipients` (#248) becomes `group_audience(event.group_id)` ∪ current `going` attendees; announcement fan-out (#68, T8) goes through it for both Announcement Audiences. Executable by no client role.
- **R27 Provisioning places the initial Groups by Appointment.** `provision_profile` takes `p_group_ids bigint[]` and `p_appointed_by uuid` and appoints through T10's shared `private.appoint_group_member`, with the inviting BC/Moderator as the actor; one refused Group fails the whole call so nobody is created half-placed. `invite-member` takes `group_ids` (not `dept_ids`/`team_ids`); `csv-import` keeps the exact header `name,email,dept,team` and resolves each value to one Group by `groups.short` **or** `groups.name`, case-insensitively and with diacritics stripped, with `team` required to sit below its row's `dept` Group. This is T25 (#602), and **T20 now waits for it** — without it the only door into the application closes the day the legacy tables drop.
- **R28 Session revocation on deactivation — open.** ADR-0003's 2026-09-20 amendment and this plan's T7 both say the revoke path is a `revoke-sessions` Edge Function called by the Administrare status editor after the command succeeds. #603's own "Correction (2026-09-21)" retires that: `@supabase/supabase-js@^2`'s `admin.signOut` takes a user's JWT, not a user id, so an administrator cannot sign someone else out; the correction puts the revoke in `private.revoke_member_sessions(p_member_id)` called from inside `set_member_status_impl`, in the same transaction. **Ruled 2026-09-21: #603's correction stands.** It is a fact about the pinned client, not a preference — there is no user-id sign-out in the v2 admin API, so the Edge-Function design cannot be built at all. T26 (#603) implements `private.revoke_member_sessions` called from inside `set_member_status_impl`; T23's closeout corrects ADR-0003's amendment, and #580's and #506's Edge-Function sentences are corrected in the issue-edit batch. T7 shipped before this ruling and cites #603 for the revoke path, which stays true.
- **R30 Applications that do not exist yet, and how a decision is recorded (ruled 2026-09-21).** Two commands settle Applications before `public.group_applications` exists: T7/#580's demotion withdraws the target's pending ones, and T9/#582's `archive_group` declines the subtree's. Neither can be written before T11/#584 creates the table, so **T11's implementer `create or replace`s both impls when it creates it**, and each command carries a `comment on function` line saying the clause is owed to #584 — recorded, never forgotten. Every row that leaves `pending` writes **both** `decided_by` and `decided_at`, as `group_applications_decision_ck` requires: the deciding Manager for an accept or a decline, the archiver for an archive-decline, and **the applicant themselves** for a self-withdrawal (they decided it). No row is ever left half-decided.
- **R31 Which `my_groups()` rows a picker may offer (ruled 2026-09-21).** `my_groups()` returns effective Group Roles including every descendant of a managed Group (R14), but membership questions use the **explicit** subset — the caller's own roster row on that Group, or Automatic Membership of it. Wave 2's ruling D2 stands unchanged: a Department member is not a member of its Child Team. So T2/#575's `hasOwnOrigin` and T4/#577's Request origin picker filter on `explicit` (plus Automatic Membership of the Group itself); Administrare's tree, the Task form's Group picker and every manager-side control use the full effective set. #575's "no broader and no narrower than today's membership test" is therefore satisfied, and the `explicit` flag is the lever that satisfies it.
- **R29 Opportunity membership is decided live, from `my_groups()`.** The first draft had T2 read the `group_ids` claim; the claim is stamped at token issue, so a Member appointed by `add_group_member` would not see their Group's local Opportunities for up to an hour. `hasOwnOrigin` now reads `my_groups()` (live, already built for the Task form and the Request picker, cached once per session). The staleness that remains — reads that follow `auth_level()` — is closed from the other side by #598's refresh on focus (adjacent, not a Wave 3 child).
- **R30 Four issues filed the same evening are not Wave 3 children:** #598 and #599 (Epic 2 — Auth & login), #600 (Task Tracker frontend, child of #90), #604 (Epic 9 — Screens). They are recorded under Deferred with their reasons; only #601, #602 and #603 join #506.
